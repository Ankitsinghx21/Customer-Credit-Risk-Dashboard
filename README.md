Customer Credit Risk Dashboard

**Project Overview**

This project analyzes customer financial data to identify credit risk and delinquency patterns using Power BI.

**Features**

KPI metrics (Customers, Loan, Credit Score, Delinquency Rate)
Risk Score calculation
Customer segmentation (High, Medium, Low risk)
Interactive filters (Age, Location, Employment Status)

**Insights**

Lower credit scores lead to higher risk
High debt-to-income ratio increases default probability
Missed payments strongly impact delinquency

**Tools Used**

Power BI
DAX
Data Modeling
